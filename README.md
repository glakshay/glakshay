<h1>👋 Hi, I'm Lakshay Gopalka</h1>

Engineer building at the intersection of AI, autonomy, and connected infrastructure. I thrive at the seam between engineering and the customer, where technical decisions have to hold up against real-world constraints and real people.

Currently at **Viasat**, architecting and deploying large-scale AI systems across satellite networks spanning 70+ countries. Previously at **Motional** (AV perception), **Alert Innovations** (warehouse robotics), and **Siemens** (industrial & service robotics), with an MS in Robotics Engineering from WPI underneath it all.

<h2>🔭 What I build</h2>

- **AI & LLM systems** — agent workflows, MCP integrations, geofencing and authentication at scale
- **Autonomous systems** — motion planning, iterative learning control, sensor fusion, perception stack validation
- **Connected infrastructure** — satellite platform APIs, telemetry pipelines, distributed fleet monitoring
- **Hardware-software integration** — ROS, embedded control, sensor characterization (LiDAR, RADAR, IMU, GNSS)
